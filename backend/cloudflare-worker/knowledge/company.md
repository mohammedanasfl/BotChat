# Company

## About Abinaya Garment Shop

Abinaya Garment Shop is a cotton apparel wholesale business based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India. It supplies quality cotton garment collections to retailers, resellers, distributors and bulk buyers.

Abinaya describes itself as a Tiruppur garment house making everyday cotton essentials for shops and brands across India, from yarn selection to the final carton, under one roof.

The business aims to make apparel sourcing simple and convenient through product variety, reliable service and direct customer communication.

## Location

Abinaya Garment Shop is located at 8/76, Angeripalayam Road, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India.

Tiruppur is one of India's leading textile manufacturing and apparel production hubs. Abinaya says its Tiruppur location and industry connections let it offer competitive wholesale prices.

Visitors can find the location on Google Maps by searching for "8/76 Angeripalayam Road, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu".

## Wholesale customer approach

Abinaya focuses on long-term partnerships with retailers, distributors and bulk buyers rather than one-off orders. Its stated priorities are:

- Premium quality cotton garments for wholesale distribution.
- A diverse product range for men, women and children.
- Flexible bulk ordering and competitive pricing.
- Direct communication and personalised service.
