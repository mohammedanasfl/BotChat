# Frequently asked questions

## How do I place a bulk order or get a quote?

Fill out the inquiry form on the website, or contact Abinaya on WhatsApp or by email with your product, quantity and requirements. On the Products page you can also add items to a quote list and send it on WhatsApp. The team replies within 24 hours on business days with a quotation and the best wholesale options, and helps you through the ordering process.

## What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

It depends on the product and any customisation. Contact Abinaya with your requirement and they will share the MOQ details. They work with businesses of all sizes.

## Can you customise products?

Yes. Abinaya offers custom printing, branding and designs on its cotton apparel. See custom printing, private label and branding under Services, and ask the team about customisation for your order.

## What payment methods do you accept?

Abinaya offers flexible payment terms for wholesale orders. Specific payment methods are not listed on the website; contact the team to discuss the payment options that work for your business.
