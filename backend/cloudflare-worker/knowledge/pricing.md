# Pricing

## Product prices

Abinaya Garment Shop does not publish product prices on its website. Prices are shared as a quotation after you send your product, quantity and requirements. To get a price, use the website's inquiry form, the quote list on the Products page, WhatsApp or email.

## Wholesale pricing approach

Abinaya describes its pricing as honest wholesale pricing: competitive rates with clear communication, clear quotes and transparent order terms. Distributors and wholesalers get direct factory pricing. Abinaya says its Tiruppur location and industry connections help it offer strong wholesale rates.

## Invoices

Abinaya provides GST invoices.
