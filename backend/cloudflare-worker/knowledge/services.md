# Services

## Who Abinaya supplies

- Retail shops and boutiques: ready-to-sell basics, steady restocks and manageable order sizes.
- Distributors and wholesalers: bulk volumes at direct factory pricing, packed for onward supply.
- Corporate and uniforms: logo-printed polos and tees for teams, events and promotions.
- Online sellers and brands: your label, your packaging, Abinaya's production, with custom branding options.

## Bulk and wholesale orders

Abinaya supports bulk orders of various sizes and says it works with businesses of all sizes. It offers dedicated support for large orders, with scalable production and dispatch support for large B2B requirements.

## Custom printing, private label and branding

Abinaya offers custom printing, branding and designs on its cotton apparel, including custom logo printing, bulk printing for branded apparel and private label (your own label and packaging). To start a custom order, share your design, product, quantity and requirements with the team.

## Manufacturing process: from yarn to your shelf

Abinaya describes an integrated Tiruppur value chain: yarn, knitting, dyeing, stitching, quality check and dispatch.

1. Raw sourcing: combed, ring-spun cotton yarn sourced from trusted Tamil Nadu spinners and checked lot by lot.
2. Circular knitting: fabric knitted in-house to the GSM you need, with tight, even stitch loops. The website lists fabric weights of 160 to 280 GSM.
3. Dyeing and washing: colour-fast, bio-washed and shrink-controlled fabric, batch after batch.
4. Cutting and stitching: precise patterns and a double-needle finish from experienced line tailors.
5. Quality check and dispatch: every piece checked, packed and shipped, with tracked dispatch across India.

## Quality

Abinaya says every garment that leaves its facility goes through quality checks, including piece-by-piece inspection and measurement checks. It works with trusted manufacturers to source premium cotton fabric. Its stated quality points are premium cotton fabric selection, rigorous quality inspections, consistent stitching and finishing, and comfortable, durable wear.

## Delivery and dispatch

Abinaya states it provides structured order tracking, reliable shipment timelines and tracked dispatch across India. Dispatch and the warehouse are closed on Sundays.
