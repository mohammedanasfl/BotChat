# Policies

## Returns, refunds, exchanges and shipping charges

The website does not publish a returns, refunds, exchanges, cancellation or shipping-charges policy. Contact the team directly for these details.

## Website privacy

Website privacy notice, last updated 18 November 2026:

- No user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or advertising trackers, and no cookies set by the website.
- Inquiry forms and the quote list don't send details to Abinaya directly: pressing send puts them into a WhatsApp message on your own device, and nothing reaches Abinaya until you send it. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.
- Details shared by WhatsApp, phone or email are used only to reply, send quotations, process and support orders, and keep business and legal records. Abinaya does not sell them and shares them only to fulfil an order or when required by law.
- The contact page's Google Maps map may let Google collect information under its own policy.
- The Products page quote list stays in your browser's local storage until you clear it.
- Details are kept only as long as needed for orders and legal records. You can ask Abinaya by email or WhatsApp to see, correct or delete your details.
