Abinaya Garments chatbot

Test playground. Open the chat button in the bottom-right corner and ask about products, orders or policies.

Embed it on any allowed website with one tag:

<script src="https://abinaya-chatbot.pages.dev/widget.js" defer></script>

Optional attributes: data-api-url, data-turnstile-site-key, data-title, data-primary-color, data-position="left".
